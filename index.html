🎯 Mission

The Church of Jesus the Glorious King (Cavite) Inc. Exists to be a part of the great commission of the Lord Jesus Christ, that is to bring all people to Him and making them His disciples (Matthew 28:19-20). And to help the church in preparing to be glorious for the meeting of the Glorious King in His second appearing to be with Him forever.

👁️ Vision

Macro Vision That all the nations of the earth will be saturated by the word of God and many will be saved regardless of color, race, and nationality, before the Lord comes. Micro Vision That the entire Philippine archipelago will be evangelized and many of the Filipino people would receive the gift of eternal life from the Lord Jesus Christ.
